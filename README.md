# ProSupply Global: Expansion Profitability Analysis

![Dashboard Overview](Dashboard%20I%20-%20Overview.png)

## Overview

Profitability analysis for a wholesale office supply company planning international expansion. Analyzed 9,994 orders to identify profit leakage and optimize discount strategy.

**Key Result:** Identified $135K in lost profits from excessive discounting. Recommended policy changes to increase margins from 12.5% to 18%.

---

## Business Problem

ProSupply Global generated $2.3M in revenue but operated at only 12.5% profit margin—below the 15-20% industry standard. Leadership needed to understand which regions, segments, and discount strategies were actually profitable before expanding into North America and Western Europe.

---

## Key Findings

### Discount Impact Analysis

| Discount Range | Profit Margin | Profit/Loss | Action |
|---------------|---------------|-------------|--------|
| No Discount | 29.5% | $321K | ✅ Maximize |
| 10-20% | 11.6% | $92K | Optimize |
| 21-30% | -10% | **-$10K** | ❌ Eliminate |
| Over 30% | -48% | **-$125K** | ❌ Eliminate |

![Additional Analysis](Additional%20Analysis.png)

**Finding:** Discounts over 20% destroyed $135K in profit (47% of potential profit).

### Geographic Performance
- West: $725K (highest revenue)
- East: $679K
- Central: $501K
- South: $392K (improvement opportunity)

---

## Recommendations

1. **Cap all discounts at 20% maximum** → Recover $135K
2. **Strengthen South region performance** → Increase by 25%
3. **Replicate West region model** for expansion into Canada (2026) and Europe (2027)

**Projected Impact:** Increase profit by 47% without growing sales.
